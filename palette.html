How to use it

  • Only navy and gold carry the brand. Don't add other colours (red, green, purple) for decoration.
  • About 60% white/ivory, 30% navy, 10% gold. Gold works best as an accent.
  • Never put gold text on white or ivory; it's too light to read. Use Royal Navy there, and Antique Gold only for small labels.
  • WhatsApp green (#25D366) is the one exception, and only on WhatsApp buttons so people recognise them.
